Common questions

What people ask most before getting in touch.

What kind of apps and systems do you build?

We build custom mobile apps, web apps, and internal tools for Norwegian businesses. Typical examples are field apps for professionals, documentation and reporting tools, customer portals, dashboards, and AI agents that automate repetitive tasks.

What does it cost to get an app built?

It varies depending on what needs to be built. We always start with a free conversation about your needs, and give you a fixed quote before we begin. No hourly-rate surprises afterward.

What's the monthly cost?

You pay a fixed monthly price that covers hosting, maintenance, and access to the solution. You can cancel whenever you want, with no lock-in or notice period.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the size. A simple field app or internal tool can be ready in a few weeks. Larger systems take longer. We're always clear about delivery time in the quote.

Do you use AI in development?

Yes, we use modern technology to build faster and better. But we're careful about what we automate: AI is used where it makes sense, and not where it requires professional judgment or precision.
